# Purpose and scope

ORANGE_PEEL, THIN_COAT, CONTAMINATION and SCRATCH findings on M-41. Change one
variable at a time and record it; three changes at once teaches you nothing.

# ORANGE_PEEL

A dimpled surface, like the skin of an orange. Almost always cure or film build.

1. Film too thick. Check thickness at five points; reduce powder flow before
   touching anything else.
2. Cure temperature too low or dwell too short. Verify the oven profile with a
   data logger, not with the setpoint on the panel.
3. Particle size drifting from over-sieved reclaim. Raise the virgin ratio.

# THIN_COAT

Coverage below specification, usually on edges and recesses.

1. Gun kV too low, or the gun too far from the part.
2. Faraday cage effect in recesses. Re-aim, or drop kV and move closer.
3. Poor earth through the hook. A dirty hook is the most common single cause -
   see the hook stripping schedule in SOP-015.

# CONTAMINATION

Specks or a colour cast in the film.

1. Incomplete cleanout after a colour change. Work through SOP-011 again.
2. Filters loaded or damaged.
3. Oil or fingerprints on the part from before the wash. Check the washer bath
   condition in SOP-013.

# SCRATCH

Almost always handling after cure, not a coating fault. Parts go into dunnage,
never stacked directly on the bench.
