# Purpose and scope

Operation of M-42 Aqueous Parts Washer, 3-Stage, on the FINISHING line. Every
part that reaches the coating booth passes through here first, so a bath out of
specification shows up as CONTAMINATION on M-41 rather than as a defect here.

# Daily checks

1. Bath temperature at each stage against the setpoint. A probe reading low is
   the usual cause of RESIDUE findings.
2. Detergent concentration by titration. Top up with CN-4002 alkaline
   degreaser; record the reading.
3. Pretreatment concentration (CN-4003 iron phosphate) and pH.
4. Spray nozzles: look down the manifold with the pump running. A blocked
   nozzle leaves an untreated stripe down one side of every basket.
5. Final rinse conductivity. High conductivity is what produces WATER_SPOT.

# RESIDUE and WATER_SPOT

- **RESIDUE**: weak detergent, low bath temperature, or overloaded baskets that
  shadow each other. Reduce the load before increasing the chemistry.
- **WATER_SPOT**: rinse water carrying dissolved solids, or parts sitting wet
  before the dryer. Check conductivity first, dryer temperature second.

# Bath change

Change the bath when concentration cannot be held or the tank is visibly
loaded. Drain, remove sludge, refill and re-titrate before the first basket.
Log the change - a bath changed and not logged looks like a bath never changed.
