# Purpose and scope

Fixture changes, job changes and first-article inspection on the MACHINING
line. Applies to M-21 through M-24.

Target: 35 minutes for a fixture swap on the 3-axis and horizontal machines,
50 minutes on the 5-axis (M-24) where probing is more involved.

# Fixture change

1. Confirm the next job and stage the fixture, tooling and program before the
   current run finishes. Staging during downtime is the single biggest lever
   on changeover time.
2. Remove the outgoing fixture, clean the table thoroughly, and inspect the
   locating features for chips. A chip under a locator is worth 0.1 mm of
   error and a scrapped first article.
3. Mount the fixture, torque clamps in sequence, and confirm clamp positions
   against the setup sheet. Do not rely on the previous shift's positions.
4. Load the program and verify the revision matches the work order.

# Work offsets and tool offsets

1. Probe the part zero. Record the values on the setup sheet.
2. Reset every tool offset from the tool setter. Carried-over offsets are the
   most common cause of a scrapped first article after a changeover.
3. Dry-run the first operation at reduced rapid to confirm clearances.

# First article

1. Run one part complete.
2. Send to CMM for full dimensional inspection. Book the CMM slot when the
   changeover starts, not when the part is ready; the queue is the usual
   reason a changeover overruns.
3. Do not start the production run until the first article is signed off.

# If the changeover overruns

Log the reason in the operator note. The recurring causes on this line are
fixture alignment reworked more than once, tooling not staged, and waiting
on the CMM. All three are preventable during the previous run.
