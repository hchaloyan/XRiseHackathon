# Purpose and scope

Colour changes on M-41 Powder Coating Booth, Automatic, on the FINISHING line.
A colour change is the largest planned stoppage this booth takes, and almost
all of the CONTAMINATION defects that follow one are traceable to an incomplete
cleanout rather than to the new powder.

Target: 30 minutes. Past 45 minutes, log the reason in the operator note.

# Cleanout procedure

1. Stop the guns and run the conveyor clear of hung parts.
2. Recover the remaining powder from the hopper into its labelled tote. Never
   return swept floor powder to a tote.
3. Blow down the booth walls, floor and roof from the top down. Working upward
   redeposits everything you have just removed.
4. Change or clean the cartridge filters. A loaded filter from the previous
   colour will bleed into the new one for the rest of the shift.
5. Wipe the guns, hoses and pick-up tubes. Replace the pick-up tube on a
   light-to-dark change.
6. Blow out the powder hose in the direction of flow, into the waste bin.

# New powder and first-off

1. Load the new powder (RM-4001 for RAL 7016 grey) and sieve it into the hopper.
2. Set the gun kV and powder flow to the values on the colour card.
3. Coat two test panels and cure them at line speed.
4. Check film thickness at five points against the specification, and inspect
   for ORANGE_PEEL and THIN_COAT before releasing the run.

# Common causes of a long changeover

- Hangers not stripped, so there is nothing clean to hang
- Filters not staged at the booth before the change started
- Recovery tote missing its label, forcing a full sieve and re-test
